HOW IT WORKS

Observe → group → alert → investigate.

The ESP32 listens for selected Wi-Fi management frames on the configured channel. It groups observed deauthentication/disassociation traffic by source and BSSID, tracks burst frequency and surfaces suspicious activity to the local dashboard.

An alert is an observation signal, not proof of attacker identity. MAC addresses can be spoofed and legitimate infrastructure can emit management frames.

Simple classic ESP32 setup.

ESP32WIRELESS DEFENSE LAB

Required: classic ESP32 DevKit / ESP32-WROOM-32 compatible board, 4 MB flash recommended, USB data cable.

Optional OLED: SSD1306 128×64 I²C at address 0x3C, SDA GPIO 21 and SCL GPIO 22.

Classic ESP32 is single-radio. The dashboard AP and passive monitor share one selected 2.4 GHz channel.
