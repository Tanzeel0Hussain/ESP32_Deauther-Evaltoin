🛡️
Capabilities

A small defensive sensor with a focused job.

📡

Passive frame monitor

Observes deauthentication and disassociation management frames on the selected channel without transmitting attack frames.

🚨

Threshold alerts

Groups events by source in a bounded time window and creates local alerts when the configured threshold is reached.

🛰️

Nearby Wi-Fi inventory

Scans SSIDs, BSSIDs, channels, RSSI and security type, then summarizes channel occupancy.

🔐

Protected administration

Mandatory first-boot password change, Digest authentication, CSRF protection and AES-GCM protected stored credentials.

📊

Local dashboard

View counters, recent alerts, scan results, free heap and uptime directly from the ESP32 management network.

🖥️

Optional OLED

Enable a 128×64 I2C status display at build time for channel, event counters and alert totals.

Defensive boundary: the maintained firmware does not include deauthentication transmission, Evil Twin impersonation, credential collection, WPA/PMKID capture, beacon flooding, BLE flooding or HID injection.
